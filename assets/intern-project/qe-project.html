Project dates: Mar 2025 to Aug 2025

How do Treasury yields and stock valuations respond to a Fed balance-sheet shock?

Modeled shock: A one-standard-deviation increase in the Fed’s balance sheet (about 9%).

Solid line: VECM estimate. Shading: 90% bootstrap interval. Dashed line: zero response.

Why VAR and VECM?

The tests suggested a long-run relationship at the preferred lag length. The VECM retains that relationship. Because the evidence was sensitive to lag choice, I used a VAR in quarterly changes as a comparison.

One limitation

The model assumes the Fed’s balance sheet does not respond to market moves within the same quarter. If the Fed responds to market stress that quickly, the estimated P/E response may partly reflect that stress rather than the effect of QE itself.
